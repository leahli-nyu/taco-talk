Method. Each Trump post on Truth Social is classified by two LLMs (Claude Haiku 4.5 + GPT-4o-mini) using a Speech Act Theory framework. A-class posts (commissive economic threats) are scored on 10 linguistic dimensions, clustered into policy episodes, and joined to formal outcomes via the PIIE Trade War Timeline. Market reactions follow standard event-study methodology (MacKinlay 1997). Full method & replication code: GitHub.

Not financial advice. Predictions reflect historical correlations, not causal claims. Sample sizes limited; treat outputs as exploratory.
