When the president posts, the tape twitches — then forgets.

We classified every tariff-relevant post on Trump's Truth Social during his second term as a speech act, isolated the commissive economic threats, matched each to a formal entry in the PIIE Trade War Timeline, and measured the S&P 500's reaction over 1, 5, and 30 trading days. The result is a quantitative ledger of bluster, backpedaling, and beta.

Cox proportional-hazards model on time-to-resolution. Loughran–McDonald "uncertainty/weak-modal" lexicon density in the post text is the strongest covariate. Preliminary & controversial — sample is small, censoring is heavy, replicate before betting on it.
